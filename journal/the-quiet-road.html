FIELD NOTE

The Quiet Road

Somewhere between leaving and arriving.

A road and a wide valley sit beneath a bright, open sky. The landscape is expansive without feeling empty, with the distant mountains providing a steady horizon and the road drawing the eye into the frame.

The story

Road photographs often work because they suggest a direction without explaining the destination. Here, the road becomes a simple line through the landscape — a visual pause between where we have been and where we might be going.

A quiet landscape study of distance, direction and open light.
