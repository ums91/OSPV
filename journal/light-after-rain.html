OBSERVATION

Light After Rain

The few minutes when sunlight breaks through the clouds and the field holds its breath.

The photograph is built around a change in light. A dark, layered sky opens just enough for sunlight to reach the field, turning an otherwise quiet landscape into a study of contrast. Birds remain small against the scene, giving the frame a sense of scale and movement.

The story

Rain changes the way a landscape reads. Colours become quieter, shadows deepen, and a short opening in the clouds can transform the whole view. This frame stays with that transition — not the storm itself, but the calm interval immediately after it.

A study of changing light, open field and the calm after weather.
