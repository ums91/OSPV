FIELD NOTE · KASHMIR

Into the Mist

A morning walk through whispers of fog and pine.

The frame holds a quiet winter landscape: dark trees gather around still water while mist softens the distance. Nothing in the scene asks for attention. The photograph works through restraint — a few layers of tone, a calm horizon and the suggestion of a larger landscape beyond the fog.

The story

Some photographs arrive as events. Others arrive as pauses. This one belongs to the second kind. It is about the brief stretch of a morning when distance disappears and familiar shapes become quieter.

A study in mist, still water and winter atmosphere.
